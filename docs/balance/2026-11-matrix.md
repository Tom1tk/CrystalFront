## Reading
The counter triangle is not there. Today's ladder is linear among the rushes (rush4 > rush > rush2 > rush1) and turtle sits on top of all of them. Turtle's turrets (cost 50, 600 HP, 3.0 dps, range 150 — longer than every unit) beat every army the rush and heavy bots send, and once the attack is spent turtle walks onto the nodes and wins on control. Macro, the only bot that contests nodes with a mixed army, is the only counter, and it wins on just 3–4 maps (the more open ones: cinder-flats, ironreed, shardfall, half of saltmarsh). Self-play on this game would very likely converge on the turtle plan, which is exactly the case the user decided to fix in the game before training.

## Why turtle wins (traces, basalt-run, seed 9001)
- **vs MediumRush:** the rush stays on 5 workers and 5–8 skirmishers for the whole game. Its attacks die around the middle; no rush unit ever gets within 600 px of turtle's crystal, which ends at 1000/1000 HP. Turtle (7 workers plus every node it holds) grows to 38 gunners and wins on control at tick 4,369.
- **vs RushBot:** 9–11 workers, but its army never grows past 13 skirmishers while it feeds them into turtle's gunners and turrets (resources stay at 0). Turtle reaches 56 gunners and wins on control at tick 5,855.
- **vs heavy:** bruisers are countered by gunners (1.5×), so turtle's gunner-only army is a hard counter.

So the dominance is mostly the scripted opponents: every rush bot builds only skirmishers, attacks piecemeal, and never contests nodes. Turret stats are not what decides these games. Macro, the one bot with a mixed army that contests nodes, beats turtle on the open maps.

## Recommendation (for the user to decide)
1. **(Recommended) No game change now; decide at T10.** T8 builds the v2 scripted bots (they contest nodes, time attacks, and switch strategy), and T10 reruns this matrix with them. That matrix is also a user-review gate before any training. If turtle still dominates there, it is the game's fault rather than the bots', and the lever experiments are rerun against opponents that can actually exploit a change. Tuning the game now against these bots would repeat the 2026-06 mistake (turrets were buffed to rescue turtle against the old rush; that buff now overshoots).
2. **Change the game now anyway.** None of the named levers works against these bots, so a change would need a different, untested lever (for example turret range 150 → 120 so turrets no longer outrange gunners, or making skirmishers better at reaching gunners). I'd test such a change against v2 bots in any case.
